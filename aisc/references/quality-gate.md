# Quality Gate

Use this file before generating the final card and before deciding whether to upload.

## Entry Recommendation

Choose exactly one:

- `长期参考`: The material contains durable concepts, methods, frameworks, data, or reasoning that can be reused months later.
- `可二创`: The material contains fresh angles, examples, stories, or expressions suitable for derivative writing or content creation.
- `仅存档`: The material has some record value but limited reusable insight.
- `不建议入库`: The material is mainly emotional amplification, anxiety marketing, repeated common sense, shallow claims, or low-quality information.

## Upload Rules

- Upload `长期参考` and `可二创` when the user requested persistence.
- Upload `仅存档` only when the user explicitly asked to save/archive.
- Do not upload `不建议入库` unless the user explicitly overrides after seeing the reason.

## Evidence Rules

- Source information must be recorded; missing fields must say `未提供`.
- Core views must be grounded in the source.
- Do not add claims, numbers, examples, author background, or conclusions absent from the source.
- Direct quotes must be copied exactly from the source. If no strong quote exists, write `无`.
- Separate facts, opinions, advice, and emotion when the source mixes them.

## Depth Rules

Generate a full learning-extension section only when the source has at least two of these:

- A clear problem and solution.
- A reusable method, process, checklist, or framework.
- Concrete examples, cases, or data.
- Explicit boundary conditions or failure modes.
- Ideas that can transfer to other scenarios.

If the source lacks enough density, create a light card and mark `仅存档` or `不建议入库`.

## Final Self-Check

Before final output, confirm:

1. The summary has exactly 3 sentences.
2. Keywords are 5-8 specific, searchable terms.
3. The source type and content category each choose one option.
4. Core views do not exceed 3.
5. Knowledge points are concrete, not generic praise.
6. Quotes are exact source excerpts or `无`.
7. Entry recommendation matches the actual material quality.
8. The card can be copied into a knowledge base without cleanup.
